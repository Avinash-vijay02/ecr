A two-day coast quest

  1. Day 1 · MorningStart at Cholamandal Artists' Village, then step into old South India at DakshinaChitra.
  2. Day 1 · AfternoonPaddle at Muttukadu Boat House, meet the crocs at Crocodile Bank, and catch the waves at Kovalam.
  3. Day 1 · EveningReach Mamallapuram for sunset at the Shore Temple and a seafood dinner. Stay the night.
  4. Day 2 · MorningDrive south past Sadras Dutch Fort and the backwaters of Alamparai Fort.
  5. Day 2 · AfternoonWatch the shimmering salt pans of Marakkanam, then visit Auroville.
  6. Day 2 · EveningEnd on the Promenade in Puducherry with a stroll through the French quarter.
