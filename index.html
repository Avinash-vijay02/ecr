Suggested two-day itinerary

A relaxed pace with time to stop along the way.

Day 1 · Morning

Cholamandal Artists' Village, then DakshinaChitra's heritage houses and crafts.

Day 1 · Afternoon

Boating at Muttukadu, the Madras Crocodile Bank, and the surf at Kovalam.

Day 1 · Evening

Sunset at the Shore Temple, seafood dinner and an overnight stay in Mamallapuram.

Day 2 · Morning

Drive south via Sadras Dutch Fort and the backwaters at Alamparai Fort.

Day 2 · Afternoon

The salt pans of Marakkanam, followed by a visit to Auroville.

Day 2 · Evening

Walk the Promenade and the French quarter in Puducherry.
